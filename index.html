What this issue would have told you [inference] If you bid road work in these four counties, the engineer's estimate was not the number to price against: the median contract cleared 7.2% under it, and the median second-place bid was 10.1% above the winner. A bid priced at the estimate was, more often than not, a losing bid. This inference fails if the next 60 contracts show a different sign or a median gap under 3%.
What it does not tell you [open] Award status, unit-price line items, change orders and rejection decisions are not in this dataset. The agency's court order or council minutes answer those. Every row here is a bid opening, not an award.

Method and limits — [fact]

  • Source. TxDOT Bid Tabulations, Texas Open Data Portal dataset de7b-7dna, data.texas.gov/dataset/Bid-Tabulations/de7b-7dna, pulled 2026-10-27. State and local lettings, contract totals, sealed engineer's estimate and bidder names.
  • Window. Let dates 2026-06-03 through 2026-10-15. Counties: Cooke, Grayson, Denton, Collin. 60 contracts with at least one bid; 23 lettings drew no bid and are excluded from the averages.
  • Definitions. Low bid is the lowest ranked responsive bid total. Gap to second is (second − low) ÷ low. Median vs. estimate is the median of (low ÷ sealed engineer's estimate − 1).
  • Material check. Grayson County 2026-03 Asphalt Materials bid tabulation, price per ton delivered, county Purchasing page, closed-status tabulation. Unit prices, not contract totals.
  • Not included. Awards, line-item unit prices, addenda, change orders, rejected bids, and anything behind a vendor registration wall. Agency records answer those.
